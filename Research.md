My research interests lie in are **psycholinguistics** and **Second Language Acquisition(SLA)**.  

Specifically, I am interested in conducting experimental research on sentence processing to explore the relationship between L1 and L2 speaker groups. Recently, I have also become interested in how this line of research can be applied to large language models (LLMs).  
Other than that, I have also done some work on my heritage language, Hakka.
